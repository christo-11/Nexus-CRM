# NEXUS CRM Support FAQ

## What is NEXUS CRM?

NEXUS CRM is the customer and operations portal for managing products, customer accounts, support tickets, meetings, and sales leads. Customers can browse active products, open support tickets, follow up on requests, attach files, and join meeting links for ticket-related discussions. Staff can review queues, manage leads, approve sales activity, and monitor team analytics.

## How do I contact support?

Sign in to the customer portal and create a support ticket. Choose Product enquiry for questions about an active product, Product defect for a product problem, or Technical support for help using the service. Tickets are routed to the relevant team and their status is visible in the customer portal.

## How do I follow up on a support request?

Open the ticket in the customer portal to read updates and exchange messages with the support team. You can also attach supported documents to a ticket and check the latest status from the portal.

## How do I reset my password or access my account?

From the login page, use the password reset flow and follow the emailed instructions. If you still cannot access the portal, contact support through a Technical support ticket and explain the account issue.

## Why can I not log in?

Common causes include a wrong email or password, a deactivated or unverified account, or a reset request that has not been completed. If the issue continues, raise a technical support ticket with your account email and the error message you see.

## How do I create or update a support ticket?

Open the customer portal, choose the relevant ticket type, provide the issue details, and submit the form. After submission, you can view updates, respond with messages, and attach files from the ticket detail page.

## What is the difference between support categories?

- Product enquiry: questions about buying, using, or understanding a product.
- Product defect: a product is malfunctioning or has a bug.
- Technical support: help with setup, access, configuration, or service usage.
- Critical tickets are escalated for rapid attention and need priority handling.

## What if the assistant cannot answer?

Use the Contact human support action in the chat. It creates a Technical support ticket in the CRM and shows the ticket reference once the CRM confirms the ticket was created.

## Can the assistant access my private account, tickets, or customer records?

No. The AI assistant answers from active product descriptions and approved public support documents. It cannot look up private account details, tickets, lead records, or another customer's information. Contact the support team through a ticket for account-specific help.

## What can I ask about NEXUS CRM?

You can ask about:
- active products and features
- setup and onboarding steps
- login, password reset, or access problems
- support tickets and follow-up steps
- meeting scheduling and ticket-linked meetings
- CRM roles and team workflows
- sales leads, verification, and approvals
- support process, policy, and escalation
- general troubleshooting and operational guidance

## What are the common NEXUS CRM workflow areas?

- Customer portal: browse products, sign in, open tickets, follow updates, attach files, and join meetings.
- Support queue: staff review and route customer issues to the correct team.
- Product catalogue: admins manage active products and descriptions.
- Leads and sales pipeline: sales managers and admins review leads, verification, and approvals.
- Meetings: tickets can include meeting links for support or operations discussions.
- Analytics and administration: admins manage users, teams, and activity reports.

## How do I create a lead?

Sign in as a Sales Manager or Main Admin and open **Leads & pipeline** from the Sales section of the workspace navigation. Select **Add lead** and enter the contact's name and work email; phone, company, product interest, and notes for the sales team are optional. Select **Create lead** to save it. A Sales Manager's lead is assigned to that manager; a Main Admin's lead is assigned to an active Sales Manager. The contact email must not already have another active lead. If the lead was created successfully, it appears in the sales pipeline.

## How do I troubleshoot a product or service issue?

Start by checking the product description and setup instructions, then confirm whether the issue is a configuration problem, access issue, or defect. If you still need help, create a support ticket with the product name, error details, and any screenshots or logs.

## How do I join or check a meeting related to my ticket?

Open the relevant ticket in the portal and look for the meeting link or scheduled meeting details. If the link is missing or not working, contact support through the ticket or ask the support team to provide an updated meeting connection.

## What should I include in a support request?

Include the product name, a short description of the issue, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, any error message, and screenshots or attachments that help the team understand the problem.

## What happens after I submit a support ticket?

The ticket is routed to the relevant department. Staff can update the case, request more information, and respond with next steps. You can monitor progress from the customer portal.

## What information is the AI allowed to answer?

The AI may answer questions based on the active product catalogue, public support documentation, and approved CRM process guidance. It may not reveal private customer data, internal staff-only records, or account-specific information that requires a signed-in user or a support agent.

## What should I do if the issue is urgent or sensitive?

Escalate it through a human support ticket or contact the appropriate support team. For account access, billing, legal, or personal information issues, do not rely on the AI assistant alone; use the support workflow.
